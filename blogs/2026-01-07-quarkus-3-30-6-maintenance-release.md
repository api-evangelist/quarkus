Today, we released Quarkus 3.30.6, a new maintenance release for our 3.30 stream, and our first release for 2026. Quarkus 3.31 will be released at the end of the month. Update To update to Quarkus 3.30, we recommend updating to the latest version of the Quarkus CLI and run: quarkus update Note that quarkus update can update your applications from any version of Quarkus (including 2.x) to Quarkus 3.30.
