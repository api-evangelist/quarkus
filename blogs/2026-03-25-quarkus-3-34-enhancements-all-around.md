Today, we released both Quarkus 3.33 LTS and Quarkus 3.34. While Quarkus 3.33 is a Long Term Support (LTS) release, branched from Quarkus 3.32 and maintained for 12 months, Quarkus 3.34 comes with a lot of enhancements across the board: Dev UI, Panache Next, and a lot of other areas got improved. This release also includes internal changes and refactorings that will bear fruits in future versions.
