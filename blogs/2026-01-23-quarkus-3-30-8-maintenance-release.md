Today, we released Quarkus 3.30.8, a new maintenance release for our 3.30 stream. It fixes a regression impacting OpenTelemetry and MDC data propagation introduced in 3.30.7. Update To update to Quarkus 3.30, we recommend updating to the latest version of the Quarkus CLI and run: quarkus update Note that quarkus update can update your applications from any version of Quarkus (including 2.x) to Quarkus 3.30.
