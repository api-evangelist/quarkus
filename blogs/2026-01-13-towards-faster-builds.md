When we initially released Quarkus, the industry was very much focused on microservices, and that was our primary target. However, Quarkus is also perfectly suited for large monoliths, whether you are migrating existing applications to a more modern runtime or building new ones from scratch. Quarkus has always been able to handle large applications, but we have recently made significant improvements in this area, particularly when it comes to build times.
