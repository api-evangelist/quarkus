Starting with Quarkus 3.36, the minimum officially supported GraalVM/Mandrel version for building native executables has been updated from 23.1 to 25.0. Why? This decision was made to reduce the maintenance overhead and focus our efforts on better supporting the latest versions of GraalVM/Mandrel.
